Model: the float is the main cylinder (h, Ø, net weight) with an optional bottom cylinder attached underneath as an independent sealed & empty section (own height h₂ and weight w₂, same diameter, no ballast inside). V = πr²(h + h₂) · displaced water (= sheet's "Required Bottle Mass") mreq = ρV · extra mass = mreq − mnet − payload · moving mass = ρVw − extra · drag uses one cross-section S = πr² since the stack shares the same diameter: trajectory integrates ma·s″ = mwg − ½ρSCx·s′|s′| (RK4, ma = displaced mass) — the same ODE written in the spreadsheet's formula row. Science constants are locked to the sheet: ρ = 998 kg/m³, Cx = 0.84, g = 9.81 m/s². Note: the numbers printed in the spreadsheet's s(t)/v(t) table come from a closed-form fit that is not consistent with that ODE (its own constants imply a ≈3.1 m/s terminal velocity, not 1.31 m/s), so trajectories here will differ from the sheet's table. Baseline values: h = 55 cm, Ø = 100 mm, net 7 kg, 550 ml.
